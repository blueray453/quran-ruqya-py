QURANIC ARABIC ANALYSIS - GENERATED CSV OUTPUTS

All CSV files in this directory are generated or copied as clearly identified reference/taxonomy tables by run_pipeline.py. Frequency tables are rebuilt from the source files bundled in data_sources.zip. Hand-curated curriculum taxonomies and output templates are copied from reference_tables.zip.

Important units:
- QUL surface frequency counts WBW word positions after removing numeric verse markers.
- QUL lemma/stem/root mappings use their independent SQLite mapping records and denominators.
- QAC morphology v0.4 supplies POS, verb features and Ism feature distributions.
- QAC Quranic.csv supplies dependency relationship and constituent annotation counts.
- MASAQ.csv supplied archive drives word/segment syntactic roles and MASAQ Ism family tables. It is not merged with QAC.
- Ism unmarked values remain unmarked; no automatic defaults are invented.
- Imperfect-verb mood uses only Muḍāriʿ occurrences. Māḍī and Amr are not assigned Indicative mood.
- The six teaching phrase patterns and the eight phrase-function families are different dimensions. QAC constituent labels and MASAQ word roles are not phrase-span/function counts.

For all methods, source versions and rights cautions, read ../README.md and ../SOURCES_AND_LICENSES.md.
