Follow the central rule: EQTB, QAC and other existing datasets are immutable reference sources. Our new dataset must contain only genuinely new phrase/clause boundaries, relationships, classifications, provenance and review decisions. Do not copy existing text, tokens, morphology, roots, lemmas, POS, dependencies, codebooks or lexicons into the new annotation dataset.

Immediate task: design and implement read-only source adapters and a reference resolver for EQTB and QAC. Then make a small Surah Al-Mulk phrase-level proof of concept. Reuse existing EQTB phrase/constituent records where they already provide the required entity. Create new annotations only for missing/incomplete information. Preserve nested and overlapping structures, and keep phrase and clause taxonomies separate.

The six EQTB auxiliary files and the user's Surah 67 summary are in this package. The full EQTB Quranic.csv and QAC morphology export are not included; obtain their paths from the user if they are needed for execution.

The scripts under prototype/ are exploratory legacy tools only. They are not the final reference-only architecture.
